HOW GRIDWAVE WORKS

every score, flag & badge explained

VAULT Score

The 0–100 number on every card. Any TD is built from a touchdown probability (see the next card). Every other category ranks a player against everyone at the same positions.

Any TD: score = 1.3 × TD% + 2 per tag + 2 if DUE (max 99.9)
Other categories: score = average of the 4 component percentiles
  • Each category has its own 4 components (listed below). Outside Any TD they're weighted equally at 25%.
  • A component bar is a percentile: 90 means better than 90% of comparable players. The number beside it is the raw stat, e.g. "53.2% · 92nd pctl".
  • Season uses every game played. Last 3 uses only the most recent three.
  • Before every team has 3 weeks of film, boards switch to a per-game Matchups view and each game's top score is re-scaled to 100 (except Any TD, which is already on a probability scale).

Any TD probability

The "% TD" on every Any TD card is the model's chance he scores a rushing or receiving TD this week.

expected TDs = usage × game script × matchup
TD% = 1 − e−expected TDs
  • Usage: expected TDs per game from where his touches happen (priced by field zone), blended 75/25 with his actual TDs per game and pulled toward the average for his role so a 2-game hot streak can't run away with it.
  • Game script: his team's Vegas implied points ÷ the league average (falls back to points per game when no line is posted).
  • Matchup: TDs this defense allows to his role (lead back, WR1, TE1…), pulled toward league average.
  • Backtested on 2026 games before launch. The Model Record tab shows how it's doing week by week.

Any TD tags

Five checks shown on every Any TD card (lit = fired, struck through = didn't). Each one that fires adds 2 to the score. "Why the tags fired" shows the numbers.

  • RZ SHARE TOP 10: his share of his team's inside-20 touches ranks top 10 at his position.
  • FAVORED: his team is favored by the spread.
  • LIGHT BOX DIET (RBs): at least 5 points more of his carries came against 6 or fewer defenders in the box than the league average. TARGET HOG (WR/TE): target share of 22%+ (WR) or 18%+ (TE).
  • DEF BOTTOM 8 VS [ROLE]: his opponent ranks in the bottom 8 for red-zone touches (carries + targets inside the 20) allowed to his role per game.
  • ACTIVE LEAK: his opponent gave up 2+ TDs to his role in its last 3 games.

Strength of schedule

The SOS chip on RB / WR / TE rows, graded on his next 4 games.

  • Each upcoming defense is graded on what it allows to his role (lead back, WR1, TE1…): yards × 0.1 + TDs × 6 per game, pulled toward league average early in the season.
  • Teams are ranked 1–32 on their next 4 games. EASY = top third, HARD = bottom third, AVG in between.
  • The player panel shows the 4 weeks as tiles (with byes), each opponent's +/- vs average, and a rest-of-season rank.

Reason line & form stat

The small text under a player's name and under his score.

  • Reason line (yellow): what's driving the score. For Any TD, up to three of: Elite Red Zone Role, High Volume, Shootout Script, Soft Matchup, Due For TD. For other categories, his two highest-ranked components, such as "Carry Share + Ground Form".
  • Form stat under the score: for Any TD, his TD probability this week (e.g. "51% TD"). For other categories, a production read such as "74.5 YDS/GM".

Flags

One flag at most per player, checked in this order. All need 4+ games played, since a few games of touchdown luck is mostly noise.

  • BREAKOUT His last 2 games beat his earlier average in EPA (by more than 1.0) and his opportunity is rising, but his season opportunity is still below the 75th percentile, so he isn't already a known workhorse.
  • DUE His TD debt is above the bar (see TD Debt). Touches say more TDs are coming. Without play-by-play loaded: top-35% opportunity but bottom-35% TD rate.
  • OVERPERFORMING Scored more than his touches support (debt below minus the bar). Expect regression. Without play-by-play: top-20% TD rate on middling opportunity.
  • SURGING / COOLING / STEADY (Rankings, when no flag): recent EPA more than +1.5 above, more than 1.5 below, or near his earlier average.

TD Debt & ⚡ CHEAT CODE

Every touch is priced by where it happened, at the league's real conversion rate from that spot (from play-by-play).

  • Zones: goal line (inside the 5), red zone (6–20), fringe (21–40), open field. Carries and targets are counted separately.
  • Expected TDs (xTD) = sum of those priced touches. TD Debt = xTD minus TDs actually scored.
  • Positive debt: the ball finds him in scoring spots more than the scoreboard shows. Negative: he has scored more than his looks support.
  • The bar for DUE / OVERPERFORMING is 2.5 − 0.15 × games played, never below 1.0, so it gets easier to trigger as the sample grows.
  • The CHEAT CODE badge needs both signals: (1) TD debt above 1.0, and (2) a real red-zone role: goal-line + red-zone share of his touches in the top 20% of his group and at least 6 of those touches on the season (3 in the last-3-games view). Click it to jump to the zone table, which shows which signals he has.
  • Needs the play-by-play file, a bigger download that finishes loading a moment after the rest.

Game insights

The lines under each game in Game Vault, like "IND has allowed 217 receiving yards to lead backs in its last 5 games, the most in the league."

  • Totals are for each defense's last 5 games, ranked across all 32 teams (1 = allowed the most).
  • Only top-5 league ranks are shown: at most 2 lines per defense, 4 per game.
  • There's no depth chart in the data, so roles are inferred per game: lead back = most carries, WR1/WR2 and TE1/TE2 = most targets, QB = most attempts.

Matchups tab

The full league table behind the game insights: what every defense allows to each role.

  • Each cell is the total over the window you pick (last 3, last 5 or full season) with the league rank beside it. Rank 1 = allowed the most.
  • Green = top 8 most generous, red = bottom 8 (stingiest). Click any header to sort.
  • Click a defense to see this week's opposing QB, lead back, top two wideouts and top tight end, each with how that defense ranks against his role.
  • Roles are inferred from usage, same as the game insights.

Usage tab

Who is gaining or losing opportunity on their team.

  • Target share (WR, TE) = his targets divided by his team's targets in each game. Touch share (RB) = his carries plus targets divided by the team's.
  • Season is the average across all his games. Last 3 is his last three games played.
  • Change = last 3 minus season, in percentage points. Green is up 5+, red is down 5+.
  • The trend line is his share game by game, ending at the yellow dot. Players need 3+ games and a minimum number of targets (15) or touches (40) to appear.
  • The Rising and Falling strips above the table show the five biggest movers each way.

TD Debt tab

The Cheat Code math as a ranked board instead of a per-player panel.

  • Due for TDs: the 15 players with the most expected TDs above TDs scored. Regression risk: the 15 who have scored furthest above what their touches support.
  • Players need 3+ games and at least 1.0 expected TDs. Switch between the full season and the last 3 games.
  • Click a player for his zone table (goal line, red zone, fringe, open field).
  • Needs the play-by-play file, so the first load is slower than the other tabs.

Red Zone leaders

Counts every carry and target a player gets inside the opponent's 20, from play-by-play. Sacks, kneels and spikes are ignored.

  • Inside 5 is the goal line. Inside 20 is the red zone, which includes the goal line.
  • Team share = his red-zone touches divided by all his team's red-zone runs and passes (green at 25%+).
  • TD per touch = TDs scored from inside the 20 divided by touches there.
  • TEAMS view: red-zone plays per game, how often they pass there, goal-line plays per game, how often they run at the goal line, and TDs per red-zone play.

Pace & pass rate

Team tendencies from play-by-play. Green means top 8 in the league for that column.

  • Plays/G: offensive snaps per game (runs, passes and sacks).
  • Pass %: dropbacks divided by all plays.
  • Neutral pass %: pass rate on 1st and 2nd down, win chance between 20% and 80%, outside the last 2 minutes of a half. This is the cleanest read on a coach's real plan.
  • Pass over expected: average gap, in percentage points, between how often the team passed and how often a typical team passes in the same spot.
  • EPA/play: expected points added per play, a quality measure of the offense.
  • Expected plays this week = the average of the two teams' plays per game.

Injury impact

Free data. Status comes from Sleeper, usage from nflverse.

  • Share left behind: his average share of team targets and carries over the last 4 games he played.
  • Who gets his work: that share is split across healthy teammates in proportion to their own current share. Carries go to running backs, targets go to everyone. Shown as extra targets or carries per game.
  • This is an estimate. A new starter or a changed game plan can beat it.
  • Players who have missed 3 or more team games sit under Out longer, because the teammates' recent numbers already include the absence.
  • Questionable players are hidden unless you tick the box.

Game weather

Free Open-Meteo forecast for the kickoff hour (the worst wind and rain over the first 3 hours).

  • WINDY 15+ mph wind or 25+ mph gusts. STRONG WIND 20+ mph or 30+ mph gusts.
  • RAIN LIKELY 60%+ chance or noticeable rain. HEAVY RAIN / SNOW are the harshest.
  • Freezing (25°F or lower) and hot (92°F or higher) games are flagged amber.
  • Domes and roofed stadiums are skipped. Neutral-site games are not mapped.
  • Wind and heavy precipitation tend to hurt passing and kicking, so they matter most for QB, WR and kicker props.

Model record

An honest report card for the Any TD board.

  • Each week is rescored using only earlier weeks of stats and play-by-play, plus that week's own Vegas spread and total, then graded against who really scored a rushing or receiving TD that week.
  • Baseline = the scoring rate of everyone on the board that week. A pick list is only useful if it beats it. 1.20x means 20% better than a random player on the board.
  • Lists tested: Vault top 10, Vault top 25, TD Debt top 10 and Cheat Code players.
  • Players who did not play are removed from the count (you would check inactives before kickoff).
  • The scoring-environment piece uses a touchdowns-per-game stand-in for team points, because past standings are not stored.
  • A few weeks is a small sample. Expect wide swings until the season is well along.

My List

  • Tap ☆ next to a player anywhere to save him. Tap ★ to remove.
  • Saved in this browser only, so it will not follow you to another phone or computer.
  • Each row: last-3-game workload, change in his share of the offense, TD debt, and how soft this week's matchup is (1 = most generous defense to that role).

Injury badges

Pulled from Sleeper's player database.

  • QUESTIONABLE stays on the board with the badge.
  • OUT / IR / PUP / Doubtful / Suspended / DNR / COVID / NA are hidden while HIDE OUT / IR / DOUBTFUL is checked. Scores are always computed on the full pool, so unchecking it never changes anyone's number.
  • Statuses can be up to 24 hours old. The "as of" time shows next to the toggle.

  • Red Zone Share — his share of his team's touches inside the 20.
  • Goal Line Opps / Game — carries + targets inside the 5 per game.
  • Implied Team Total — his team's Vegas implied points this week.

These bars show the inputs. The Any TD score itself comes from the TD probability and tags above.

QBs are left out on purpose: a TD pass isn't "scoring" in this market.

PASS YARDS QB

  • Air Volume — passing air yards per game.
  • Secondary Leak — pass yards the opponent allows per game.
  • Script Lean — how pass-heavy his team is versus the league average.
  • Environment — his team's points per game.

PASS TDS QB

  • Red Zone Passing — pass TDs per attempt.
  • Air Volume — passing air yards per game.
  • Secondary Leak — pass yards the opponent allows per game.
  • Script Lean — pass-heavy versus league average.

RUSH YARDS RB · QB

  • Carry Share — his share of his team's carries.
  • Box Count Diet — rush yards the opponent allows per game. A proxy, not charted defenders-in-the-box.
  • Script Lean — rewards run-heavy teams (the inverse of pass lean).
  • Ground Form — his rush yards per game.

RECEPTIONS WR · TE · RB

  • Target Design — his share of his team's targets.
  • Hands — receptions per target (catch rate).
  • Coverage Leak — receiving yards the opponent allows per game.
  • Script Lean — pass-heavy versus league average.

REC YARDS WR · TE · RB

  • Target Design — his share of his team's targets.
  • Yards / Target — receiving yards per target.
  • Coverage Leak — receiving yards the opponent allows per game.
  • Script Lean — pass-heavy versus league average.

Separate from Receptions on purpose: a player can clear the catches number and miss the yards number.

DATA & LIMITS

what each number is, and what it isn't

Where the data comes from

  • nflverse — weekly player stats and play-by-play (stats, TD Debt, headshot fallbacks).
  • ESPN — scoreboard, standings, schedule and player headshots.
  • Sleeper — rosters, injuries and ESPN player IDs.
  • Open-Meteo — venue weather. The Odds API — game lines (Markets).

Research chart & game log

  • Bars are green or red against the player's own season average, not a sportsbook line. GRIDWAVE has no per-player prop-line source yet.
  • The game log shows week, opponent and the stat only, with no W/L or final score yet.
  • Section headers in a player's panel collapse and open with the chevron.

Not included (yet)

  • Real box counts and coverage shells (light/stacked boxes, single-high vs two-high) need charted pre-snap data. It isn't in the free sources, so Box Count Diet uses rush yards allowed instead.
  • Per-player prop lines — the free odds tier covers game spreads, totals and moneylines only.
  • Name matching between Sleeper and nflverse is done by name and position, so a rare mismatch can drop an injury tag or a photo.

HOW THE NBA MODEL WORKS

every number, explained

Projection = minutes × per-minute rate

Every projected stat is built from two pieces that are blended separately: how many minutes he'll play, and how much he produces per minute. PRA is the sum of the points, rebounds and assists projections.

blend = (n × this season + k × last season) ÷ (n + k)

n is games he's played this season. k is how many games' worth of trust last season gets. At n = k it's 50/50, and the weight slides toward this season on its own as games come in.

Why each stat has its own k

  • Minutes · k = 3. Role changes (new starter, trade, new coach) show up immediately.
  • Rebounds, assists · k = 10. Fairly stable, moderate noise.
  • Points · k = 12. Depends on shooting, which is noisy early.
  • Threes made · k = 15. The noisiest of the four, so last season holds on longest.

The chip on each player shows where he is: PRIOR no games yet, EARLY under 5 games, BLEND 5–14, CURRENT 15+.

Edge cases

  • New team. Last season's per-minute rates travel with him, but last season's minutes don't. Until he plays, his old minutes are trimmed 10%.
  • Rookies / no NBA history. The prior is the league average for his position (guard, forward, center), and k is halved so his own games take over twice as fast. Minutes start at 14.
  • Small samples last season. Every prior gets 250 minutes of position-average production mixed in, so 12 games last year can't act like 70.

Game-day adjustments

  • Teammates out. Minutes from players listed OUT or DOUBTFUL (10+ MPG) are handed to available teammates in proportion to their roles, capped at +25% each and 40 minutes total.
  • Back-to-back. Teams on the second night of a back-to-back get minutes trimmed 4%.
  • Questionable / day-to-day players are flagged but still projected. Check status before tip.

Matchups (defense vs position)

For each defense: how much it gives up to guards, forwards and centers, per minute, compared with the league rate for that position. +10% means it allows 10% more than average.

  • Per minute, so a team whose starters play heavy minutes doesn't look softer than it is.
  • Blended like the players: this season's games against last season's, with k = 10 team games (rosters turn over, so last season fades fast).
  • GREAT MATCHUP one of the 5 softest defenses against his position for the stat you're viewing, and at least +5%. TOUGH MATCHUP one of the 5 stingiest, at least −5%.
  • Matchups are shown next to the projection, not baked into it, so you can see both numbers separately.

Data sources

  • ESPN: scoreboard, rosters, injuries, and this season's box scores.
  • sportsdataverse (hoopR): last season's full regular-season box scores, the prior.
  • ESPN (DraftKings): player prop lines, free. The Odds API is the backup when ESPN has none for a game.

Limitations

  • Matchup ratings are shown beside the projection but don't change it, and there's no pace adjustment yet.
  • Late scratches only show once ESPN updates the injury report. Lineups confirmed 30 minutes before tip beat any model.
  • Once the season is more than 30 days old, "this season" means his last 30 days of games, which is also the more relevant sample.
  • Name matching between the sportsbooks and ESPN can occasionally miss a player with an unusual spelling.
